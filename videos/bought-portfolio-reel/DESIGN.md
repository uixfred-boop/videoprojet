# DESIGN.md

Brand truth for this project is the repo style **`styles/listicle-crimson/STYLE.md`** (listicle hub, crimson glass).
Components come from `assets/css/components.css` (a copy of `styles/listicle-crimson/components.css`), plus
`assets/css/house.css` for this film's extra pieces.

Key tokens:

- background `#0B0B0B` with a 40px grid at 4%;
- crimson `#99122B` (highlight `#B8193A`);
- node yellow `#F5C518` (rare);
- glass `#232325 → #111113` with a 16% white edge;
- ink `#F5F5F5`;
- ghost `#6B6B6B`;
- Inter Tight 800 plus Instrument Serif Italic.

The layout keeps the house split: graphics y 150–1180, caption seam at 1277, footage card x 72–1008 from y 1410 with
the head pop-out, and full-screen cutaways on the emotional lines.
