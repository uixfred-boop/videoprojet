## Intent

Second reel in the series. A first-person confession: "I cheated, I bought a ready-made portfolio". Mass
outreach, 500 messages, one $350 Shopify job, the realisation that a copied portfolio proves nothing, then
locking in and studying the best portfolios. The user asked for the edit "in this reference style", which is
`styles/listicle-crimson/STYLE.md` (the repo's default). They also asked for "a dynamic instrumental beat that fits,
and if that's not possible, leave it, I'll do it myself".
