## Intent

A first-person Instagram Reel. The creator made $7,000 on one web design project after years of views that paid
nothing, and after Fiverr, Upwork and cold email all failed. The voice-over carries the story. On screen it is motion
design that follows the script beat by beat. The user asked for the motion to follow the three reference reels
"exactly", so the format is their split layout:

- graphics panel on top (headline, dark glass cards);
- 1–3 word captions on the seam;
- B-roll card at the bottom, with the subject's head breaking out of its frame;
- occasional full-screen cutaways at emotional beats.

## Assets

- assets/audio/voiceover.mp3: the user's voice-over (63.3s). Source of truth for timing; words aligned in `transcript.json`.
- assets/video/broll.mp4: the user's B-roll at the laptop (720x1280, 26.8s, silent). Used as the bottom strip and for the full-screen cutaways.
- refs (not shipped): three Instagram reels from the user, analysed frame by frame. Their house style is written down in DESIGN.md.
- script00.docx: the script. The voice-over follows it word for word.

## Customizations

- Count-ups on the money stats ($7,000, views, $0.00).
- Captions are the references' word-group captions. Key phrases switch to the italic serif.
- SFX from the bundled media-use library (whoosh, pop, click, impact, typing). A quiet music bed, because the references carry one.

## Notes

- The run shape was inferred. The user sent the references without picking "plan first" or "straight to video". Their
  original ask was for the finished edit, so this runs without a storyboard pause.
- Don't invent statistics. Figures on screen are either from the script ($7,000; millions of views; $0) or deliberately
  blurred placeholders.
