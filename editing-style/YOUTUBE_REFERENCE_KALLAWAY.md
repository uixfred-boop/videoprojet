# YouTube Long-Form Reference — "You Will Hate This, But It'll Grow Your Social Media 10x Faster" (Kallaway)

This is the house style for long-form YouTube. It replaces the vertical "cozy editorial" guide for anything
16:9. Everything below was measured on the reference: 20 min 28 s, 1280×720 at 59.94 fps, 4,680 words.

---

## 1. The formula in one paragraph
This is a list video: a **hook**, then **"N ways"**, each delivered as *talking head → full-screen animated explainer → talking head*,
then a **soft CTA**. The pace is fast: **229 words per minute**, and no pause anywhere is longer than 0.3 s. Heavily compressed voice sits on a barely
audible music bed. Everything visual lives in one **dark navy / electric-blue "glass UI"** design system. Nothing is random, and there are no
memes or stock footage. Every graphic either **lists, compares, maps or proves** what is being said.

## 3. Picture & rhythm
- **Talking head:** 58 % of runtime, in 35 blocks (median 16 s, 90th percentile 45 s).
- **Full-screen graphics, screen recordings or B-roll:** 42 %, in 33 blocks (median 11 s, 90th percentile 42 s). The two **alternate**: you never get more than about 45 s of plain face.
- **Three framings** on the talking head, measured from the background scale:
  - **Wide:** the base frame, used about 60 % of the time.
  - **Medium:** about +10 %.
  - **Tight:** about +14 %.

  The framing switches **on a cut** about every **14 s** of talking head, and never twice in a row at the same level.
- **Slow push-ins:** about 23 in the video, each roughly +5 % over 3–6 s, used during a build-up sentence.
- **Jump cuts:** the hidden ones are between every sentence (no pauses survive). The visible ones are reframes.
- **Set:** dark room, moody. Cyan vertical LED bar camera-right, framed B&W posters camera-left, shelves with warm practical bulbs, cap + black hoodie, podcast mic in shot.

  > Fred's episode-2 background plate (posters, shelves, cyan LED) is already this set. Use it.
- **Grade:** low key, crushed but detailed blacks, cool shadows (teal/navy), warm practical highlights, skin kept natural.

## 4. Graphic design system ("blue glass UI")
- **Background for full-screen graphics:** near-black navy `#0B1220` → deep blue `#12305E`, with a soft radial **blue glow** (`#2F6BFF` at about 25 %) from the bottom or a corner. Fine film grain. No pure black.
- **Lines and outlines:** 1–2 px, light blue-white `#CFE0FF` with an outer glow. Nodes are small glowing dots.
- **Cards ("glass"):** rounded rectangle (radius about 10 px at 720p), fill navy at 60–80 % with a top-light gradient, 1 px light-blue border, soft outer glow, backdrop slightly blurred. Titles bold, body regular.
- **Typography:**
  - **Font:** a geometric grotesk (Inter / SF Pro feel). Titles **bold, white**; secondary text **60 % white**. Quotes and examples in *italic*.
  - **Highlighted key words:** bold, with a pill highlight in electric blue `#3D7BFF`.
  - **Titles with a glow:** for example "6 Ways To Grow Faster", "The 4 S's of Hookwriting", "The Idea Problems:". Words **reveal left to right**, grey to white.
- **Motion vocabulary:**
  - Elements **build in sequence with the voice**: list rows, tree branches, flow nodes connecting with an animated line.
  - Easing is smooth (ease-out, 250–400 ms).
  - Slight **3D parallax / float** on cards.
  - A **camera move inside the graphic**: slow zoom into the item being discussed.
- **Recurring templates:**

| Template | Used for | Example in reference |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter grid | List overview and progress, at the start of every item | 0:24, 4:36, 8:48, 11:52, 15:16, 18:00, 19:52 |
| Tree / branch list | One concept broken into parts | "Visual Style" with 6 rows (2:12–3:20) |
| Text panel + head cutout | Showing examples or scripts: italic text cards left, small glass portrait of the speaker right | "Example: The easiest way…" (5:36–7:08) |
| Floating cards around the head | Proof / social: stat badges, avatars, screenshots, phone mockups floating on both sides while he keeps talking | 0:13, 0:36, 1:08, 3:36 |
| Lower-third "Takeaway" | One-sentence summary at the end of a section | 4:24, 11:36 |
| Side panel list | Bullet list building on the right third while the speaker sits left | "Ideas are:" / "The Idea Problems:" (12:04–12:44) |
| Flow diagram | Systems and workflows: nodes plus animated connectors | 16:04–16:24, 17:16 |
| Abstract data viz | Concepts: network, pie, cylinder, dots converging | 1:44, 10:16–11:16 |
| Screen recordings | Real tools and proof, in a browser frame with rounded corners and a glow, on a blue background | 13:08–14:12, 17:40 |
| Name tag | "@username" or name, a small glass pill near the bottom | 16:28, 20:16 |

## 5. Sound
- **Voice:** −14.2 LUFS integrated, **loudness range 1.8 LU** (very compressed, broadcast-tight), with a clean noise floor.
- **Music bed:** under about 70 % of the video, about 25 dB below the voice. Ambient/electronic, never distracting, stereo (the voice is mono).
- **Sound effects:** about 44 in 20 min, roughly one every 30 s. Soft whooshes and risers on **transitions into full-screen graphics**, subtle UI clicks or blips on element reveals. Nothing cartoonish.

## 6. Rules for Fred's edit
1. **Background:** episode-2 room plate (it's the same set language), person matted and centred.
2. **Pauses:** cut all of them. Target 190–230 wpm without making Fred sound rushed.
3. **Framing:** reframe between wide, medium (+10 %) and tight (+14 %) on cuts, about every 14 s. Add a few slow push-ins on build-ups. Shoot in **4K** so the tight framing stays sharp.
4. **Graphics:** every list, comparison, number, quote or system gets a graphic from the template table. Keep plain talking-head runs **≤ 45 s**.
5. **Chapter grid:** one per script section, with progress states.
6. **Proof:** use real screen recordings and screenshots (portfolio site, client project, Instagram stats) in browser frames. These must come from Fred.
7. **Mix:** voice at −14 LUFS, compressed; music bed −25 dB under the voice; whoosh on full-screen transitions, soft clicks on reveals.
8. **No:** memes, stock footage, emoji stickers, big bouncing captions or outro cards.
